# Tochii Achebe

**Founder and CEO of Amakora · London**

I build software for the work that falls between tools, teams, and decisions. At Amakora, that means connecting the systems people already use and making it clear what the software did, what needed approval, and what happened next.

My background is in payments, developer platforms, and commerce infrastructure at Interswitch and RedCloud. I’m a Google AI Fellow (2026), and my work spans product strategy, platform architecture, and hands-on engineering.
